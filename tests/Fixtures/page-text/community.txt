Chapter
IX
Community
A private circle of colleagues and former students.
The Scientific Community is a private space for researchers, former students and colleagues of Professor Khidir to keep in touch, share work in progress and continue the conversations he began. Membership is by application and every application is read by an administrator.
Who may join
Who may join
Academics and practitioners in agriculture and the related sciences, former students of Professor Khidir, and colleagues from the institutions where he taught and served. Please tell us briefly who you are and why you would like to join.
Conduct
Conduct
01
Be courteous. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
02
Keep discussion to scholarship, teaching and the life and work of Professor Khidir.
03
Share only material you have the right to share, and credit its authors.
04
Do not post advertising, fundraising or personal data about others.
05
Messages may be reported to the administrators, who may remove them and suspend members who break these rules.
Privacy
Privacy
Your name, email address, affiliation, academic field and reason for joining are used only to review your application and, if it is approved, to show your name and affiliation beside your messages to other members. They are kept while your account exists and are never published, sold or shared outside the site. Administrators can see your application; other members see only your name and affiliation. You can ask for your account to be removed at any time by writing to the site administrator.

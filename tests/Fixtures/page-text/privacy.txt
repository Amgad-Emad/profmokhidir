Privacy
How this site counts its readers
To learn which pages are read, this site keeps its own simple visitor count. It is run by the site itself: no other company sees it, and nothing is shared or sold.
What is counted
The page opened, and the site it was reached from (only its name, such as a search engine).
How long the page was read: the time the tab was in view while you scrolled or used the page within the last 30 seconds, and how far down you scrolled.
The country you are in, worked out from your internet address, which is then forgotten. Nothing more precise than the country is kept.
Whether you use a phone, tablet or computer, and the name of the browser.
What is not
No cookies are set and nothing is stored in your browser for this count.
No internet address, name, email or other personal detail is stored. Visits on the same day are told apart by a scrambled code that changes every day and cannot be turned back into anything about you.
If your browser asks sites not to track you (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), nothing is counted at all.
How long it is kept
The record of each page view is deleted after 30 days. After that only daily totals remain (for example, how many people read a page on a given day), and those are deleted after 25 months.
